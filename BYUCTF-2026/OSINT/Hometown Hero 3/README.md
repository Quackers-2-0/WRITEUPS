# Solution

We know the location of the house Kearney, Nebraska. Now find the exact house of Cameron Snider childhood house. Go to his Quora account, https://www.quora.com/profile/Cameron-Snider-5/, under following, Cameron Snider follows 2 people Daren Snider and Colin Snider. One is either brother or father. Went to Realtyhop, https://www.realtyhop.com/property-records/search, to search either names for property listings. It was Daren Snider assuming his father, find the property listed in Kearney Nebraska and found 4103 Fairacres Road, Kearney, NE 68845. The website price was hidden so had to find another website, found using zillow, https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/4103-Fairacres-Rd-Kearney-NE-68845/111735395_zpid/ and scroll down to the recent record 10/26/2018.
