# Description

Well, that didn't take long. Lets try a more personal one.
When Cameron was in highscool, he had to learn a foreign
language. Can you figure out what language that was?

Format: byuctf{Language} Example: byuctf{Welsh}

# Solution

Search up Cameron Snider on Google, found his LinkedIn, personal website, GitHub, Instagram and Quora account. 
Go to his LinkedIn account and find his high school. Richmond High School. 
Go to the school's website, https://www.werrichmond.com/. Under, 'Academics', 'Curriculum', scroll all the way down to 'Curriculum: RHS (9-12)' and click the link and go to 'World Languages'. 
There is 2 languages listed, Spanish and French. It was French.
